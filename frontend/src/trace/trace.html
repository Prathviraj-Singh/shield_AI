    What is this? Police receive cyber-fraud complaints together with phone-call records (CDR), internet session logs (IPDR) and bank/UPI settlement records. This tool links those files automatically and shows where the stolen money went — all inside this browser tab, nothing uploaded.
    CDR = call records  ·  IPDR = internet session records  ·  IMEI = phone handset ID  ·  IMSI = SIM card ID  ·  UPI handle = payment ID (e.g. name@bank)  ·  /24 network = block of nearby internet addresses
    1. Load the sample case, or drop your own CDR, IPDR and bank/UPI settlement CSVs.
    2. Read the money-flow graph from victim to cash-out.
    3. Print the one-page brief or download it as JSON.

    Expected columns — CDR: caller, callee, timestamp, duration_sec, imei, imsi, cli_type. Bank: txn_id, timestamp, from_upi, from_mobile, to_upi, to_mobile, amount, channel. IPDR: msisdn, timestamp, src_ip, imei.

    How to read this page
    • Arrows = money direction. Solid arrows show fund transfers; the amount is printed on the arrow. Thicker arrow = more money.
    • Colours: Blue = victim, Red = high risk (score 60+), Amber = medium risk (30–59), Grey = low risk.
    • Click any node to see exactly why it got its risk score.
    • "Prime suspects" and "Immediate action" in the brief below are the outputs an investigating officer acts on.
